iOS Icon Pack gives supported Android launchers an iOS-inspired look across six visual eras: iOS 14 through iOS 18, plus iOS 26 Liquid Glass.

The catalog includes 342 era variants and 25 exact icons for popular third-party apps. A total of 671 component mappings connect the artwork to common apps and Android system tools. Every catalog icon also includes a monochrome vector for Material You themes and a transparent glyph variant.

Six original wallpapers are included. Preview them full screen, save a copy, or apply one to your home screen from the app.

Highlights:
• Browse and compare all six icon eras
• Search the full 367-icon catalog
• Apply the pack through supported launcher shortcuts
• Send a structured request when an app is missing
• Use an AMOLED dark dashboard with no ads

The app has no analytics, in-app purchases, or license checks. It is open source under the MIT License.
